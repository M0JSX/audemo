AUDEMO -> AUDITION TEST PACK (Audemo 1.7)
==========================================

Six multitrack sessions saved by Audemo as Audition .sesx files, with the
audio they use in the Audio folder. Keep the folder structure as it is.

For each session: open it in Audition (File > Open), compare what you see
with the list below, and note anything that's different. Then:

  1. File > Save As, in the same folder, adding " (Audition)" to the name,
     for example "04 Clip envelopes (Audition).sesx".
  2. Also please make two small sessions in Audition itself and save them
     here (these show exactly how Audition writes things Audemo can't
     check by itself):
       a) "07 Made in Audition.sesx": one track, one clip that you trim from
          the left so it starts part-way into its file, with a few clip
          volume keyframes and a few clip pan keyframes (View > Show Clip
          Volume/Pan Envelopes), plus a few track volume keyframes.
       b) "08 Surround.sesx": a new 5.1 session (File > New > Multitrack
          Session, Master: 5.1) with one stereo track and one 5.1 track if
          you have a 5.1 file, else any file. Move a track's surround
          panner off centre. Then a 7.1 one if your Audition version has it.
  3. Zip the folder (the Audio folder isn't needed) and send it back.

Track names describe what each track should do, and clip names what each
clip's envelope does. Times are session times in seconds unless noted.


01 Basics
---------
[ ] Opens without errors or missing-file warnings.
[ ] "Vol -6 Pan L50": volume -6 dB, pan L50.
[ ] "Vol +3 Pan R100": volume +3 dB, pan R100; its clip has clip gain -6 dB.
[ ] "Muted": track muted; clip "Clip gain +4.5" has +4.5 dB clip gain.
[ ] "Solo": track soloed; clip "Muted clip" is muted.
[ ] Clips start at 0 s, 4 s, 0 s and 2 s.

02 Fades
--------
[ ] Five tracks, each a clip at 1 s with a 2 s fade in and 1.5 s fade out.
[ ] Fade shapes match the track names: linear; log +50; log -50; log +100;
    cosine. (Hover a fade handle to read its curve value.)

03 Track automation
-------------------
[ ] "Volume 0 > -20 > +6 dB": volume keyframes at 1 s (0 dB), 3 s (-20),
    5 s (-20) and 7 s (+6 dB).
[ ] "Pan L100 > R100": pan keyframes at 1 s (L100), 4 s (R100), 5 s (C).
[ ] Playing it, the tone dips and swells and the noise sweeps left to right.

04 Clip envelopes
-----------------
The key question here is whether the keyframes sit at the right place,
especially on the clip that starts part-way into its file.
[ ] "Clip volume (untrimmed)": clip at 1 s; its volume envelope dips to
    -18 dB between 2 s and 4 s into the clip (session 3 s to 5 s), with
    half-second ramps.
[ ] "Clip volume (trimmed start)": clip from 3 s to 7 s showing clicks
    from 2 s into the file; its volume rises from silence at the clip's
    start (3 s) to 0 dB one second later (4 s). The click at 3 s should be
    silent and the click at 4 s at full level.
[ ] "Clip pan L > R": clip at 2 s; pan envelope from L100 at its start to
    R100 five seconds in (session 7 s).

05 Markers and tempo
--------------------
[ ] Markers panel shows: "Cue at 1 s" (cue, with description "A plain cue
    marker"); "Range 3-5 s" (a range); "CD track at 6 s" (CD track);
    "Subclip 8-12 s" (subclip, 4 s long).
[ ] Tempo 100 bpm, 3 beats per bar (Bars and Beats time display).

06 Buses and loops
------------------
[ ] "Bus -6 dB" is a bus track at -6 dB.
[ ] Both other tracks output to "Bus -6 dB".
[ ] "Into bus (looped clip)": a 4 s clip of clicks, looped out to 20 s.
[ ] "Into bus (locked clip)": clip at 2 s, Lock in Time on.
